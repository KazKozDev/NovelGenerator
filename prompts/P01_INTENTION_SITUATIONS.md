# P01_INTENTION_SITUATIONS — pressure on independent intentions

Build three pressure situations from this book's causal structure: an early
commitment, a consequential reversal, and the decisive choice near the ending.
Use the actual people and causal events, not invented obstacles. Include every
person whose discretionary response is necessary for the tested result, including
institutional decision makers. Do not replace their choices with automatic success.
A missing decisive person can be reported later as a structural defect; never
invent a cast id or assign an official's authority to an existing unrelated person. In a solitary or
quiet story use competing desires, obligations or interpretations; do not invent
an antagonist or demand an action thriller. Each situation must allow at least
two plausible choices and have consequences for the next events.
