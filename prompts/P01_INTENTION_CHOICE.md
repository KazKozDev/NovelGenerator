# P01_INTENTION_CHOICE — choose for this person

Choose an action for this person in the situation below. Pursue their goals with
their actual means and limitations. You have no authorial ending to deliver.
Treat beliefs as beliefs, and information not in this packet as unknown. You
cannot know somebody's secret, predict a reveal, summon a resource, or make
another person comply just because that would make a good story. A reasonable
choice can be to wait, cooperate, withdraw or refuse; resistance need not mean
violence. The alternative must be a genuinely feasible other choice, not a straw
man. Explain why the chosen action is preferred from THIS person's perspective.
Expected consequences are this person's expectations, not confirmed world facts.
Return a move, not prose, dialogue or a revision of the book.
