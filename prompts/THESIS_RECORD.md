You keep the continuity sheet of a novel while it is being written. A scene has just been written. Take down from it only what later scenes must agree with. You are copying out what the text says, not judging it and not improving it.

WHAT THE SHEET ALREADY HOLDS:
{{record}}

THE SCENE JUST WRITTEN ({{id}}; the outline placed it at: {{when}}; {{where}}):
{{text}}

Return exactly this JSON and nothing else:
{"day":"The day and time of this scene as the text gives it, and how long after the previous scene, in a few words",
 "facts":[{"about":"A person, thing or place, by the name the text uses","fact":"One particular the text states that could be contradicted later"}],
 "shown":["One line: something the reader has now been shown in full and need not be shown again"]}

What belongs in facts: how each person is addressed and titled; full names, relatives, pets, employers; ages, dates, durations and counts the text commits to; what an object looks like, where it is kept and who has it at the end of the scene; what someone has promised, agreed to or lied about, and to whom; what someone now knows.
What does not: moods, weather, anything already on the sheet in the same words, anything the text only implies.
shown is for set pieces: a hiding place described, a room described, a routine walked through, an argument had, a discovery made. Two to five lines.
Use the text's own names and numbers exactly. At most fourteen facts, the ones most likely to be got wrong later.
