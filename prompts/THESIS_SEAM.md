Read the end of the first scene and the start of the second together, as one place where a reader crosses. Check only the crossing:
- where each person is, and how they got from where the first scene left them;
- how much time has passed, and whether the text lets the reader know;
- what is in whose hands, and the state anyone's body was left in;
- an action left unfinished that the next scene ignores, or finishes twice;
- something said or revealed at the end of the first that the second treats as unsaid or unknown;
- an entrance, exit or arrival made twice.

A cut in time or place is allowed when the reader can follow it; do not ask for a narrated walk between every two rooms.
