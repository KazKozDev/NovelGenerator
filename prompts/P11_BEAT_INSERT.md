RULES
- At most {{max_words}} words, in one to three paragraphs.
- Establish it through something done, noticed, said, or decided — never by
  the narrator announcing it.
- Nothing may happen that the paragraph after it does not already allow:
  nobody arrives or leaves, nothing changes hands, no time passes that the
  next paragraph does not account for.
- Use only people, places and things the scene already holds. Every name on
  record is spelled exactly as shown.
- Do not repeat what the paragraph before it says, and do not anticipate what
  the paragraph after it says.
