# P01_READER_TRACES
Construct the evidence a reader will encounter, in chapter order, from the approved
plan and intention collisions. For every chapter give concrete visible traces left
by characters' actions, the hero's price for discovering them, and the new difficulty.
Do not simply announce the author's explanation. Do not invent events or capabilities.
A source event may be hidden past only when this chapter's on-page action actually
exposes a trace of it. Explain that route. Do not expose later revelations early.
No obligatory red herrings, twists or mysteries for genres that do not need them.

Exactly one beat per chapter, at least one trace each. Trace IDs unique book-wide.

Visible facts include observable choices and outcomes, not just objects. At the
climax show the decisive action already specified by the plan, its refusal or
acceptance, and the resulting cost. Showing an offer alone does not show whether
it was accepted. Do not omit the observable ending action from the final beat.
