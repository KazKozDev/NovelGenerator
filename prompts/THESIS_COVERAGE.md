For every given, say where the book makes it happen in front of the reader. A scene makes something happen when that thing is the scene's own action or outcome. It does not happen on the page when it is only state_before, backstory, a summary of several occasions inside another event, something a character reports afterwards, or a conclusion the narration states.

Return one entry per given index, each index exactly once:
{"coverage":[{"given":0,"kind":"story","thesis_id":"an actual thesis ID","field":"action","note":"The words of that field that are this event"}]}

kind is "requirement" for an instruction about form (voice, tense, tone, length, chapter count, ending type) and "story" for anything that is or was the case in the story, including a standing habit or repeated situation: a habit is on the page when at least one occasion of it is a scene's action.
field is "action" or "outcome" when the given is that field of thesis_id; "state_before" when the nearest thing the outline has is background, aftermath or report; "none" when the outline does not contain it at all.
thesis_id is the scene that owns the given, or, for "state_before" and "none", the scene where it would have to happen. For a requirement give any thesis ID and field "none".
Judge the fields as written. Do not credit a scene with what a writer might add.
